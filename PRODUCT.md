# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring teams at companies evaluating Juan Villaverde as a software developer. They scan quickly, want to see who he is, what he has built and done, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Juan Villaverde, software developer (UDC graduate, pursuing the Master's in Computer Engineering at UDC). Success means a recruiter understands his profile, sees his projects and experience, and gets in touch (contact form, email, LinkedIn).

## Positioning
A developer portfolio whose form itself shows craft and personality; the content stays factual and modest.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 4, deployed to GitHub Pages at juanvillaver.de (public/CNAME).
- Bilingual ES (default) / EN via i18next; keys in src/i18n/locales. `<mark>` tags highlight key phrases.
- Light/dark theme toggle persisted in localStorage.
- Contact form sends through EmailJS using VITE_EMAILJS_* env vars.
- Sections: profile, experience, projects, certifications, contact, footer.
- Google AdSense account meta tag in index.html must stay.

## Brand Commitments
- Content meaning must be preserved; structure, ordering and phrasing may change freely (user, 2026-10-21).
- Redesign direction pinned by the user: entirely ASCII-art based.
- Real images (profile photo, project screenshots) are shown converted to ASCII; certificate PDFs are linked, not previewed.

## Evidence on Hand
- Profile photo: public/assets/Yo.jpg
- Projects: Ocnet synth (TFG, grade 9.0, C++/JUCE), Bus Pontevedra (Kotlin/React/Tailwind), VuelingConnect (Flutter/Python, BLE mesh). Screenshots in public/assets.
- Experience: private C tutor on Tus Clases Particulares (Nov 2024 – Feb 2025); personal projects (ongoing).
- Certifications: Power BI course (Santander, Sep 2024), Cambridge C1 English (Mar 2025); PDFs in public/assets.
- No testimonials, metrics or employers beyond these; do not fabricate.

## Product Principles
- Recruiter can grasp the profile in seconds; personality never blocks scanning.
- Every fact on the page is real and sourced from the existing content.
- Contact is always one step away.
